Featured Project

SPUD - Accessible Gaming Controller

SPUD is an innovative gaming controller designed to improve accessibility in gaming. It focuses on being affordable, compact, and versatile so that players can enjoy one-handed gameplay without sacrificing functionality or performance.

Developed as a capstone project at the University of Colorado Boulder by Adam Millman and Kai Franks, SPUD combines ergonomic design with customizable controls and motion input, integrating seamlessly with existing gaming platforms.
